Seven-step licence journey

01

Discover

Review publicly approved products and capabilities.

02

Configure

Build a client-specific schedule: scope, users, devices and term.

03

Verify rights

Check component licensing, provider permissions and contractual authority.

04

Approve

Complete commercial approvals, pricing and agreement signatures.

05

Admit

Request Warden authorization for the bounded client estate.

06

Activate

Register with Genesis and orchestrate approved deployment work.

07

Evidence & renew

Maintain River receipts, metering, service assurance and renewals.
